# Palette inspection

The badge and `design/pet-badge-concept.html` share a 1024 × 600 layout: one main photograph and three matching photographs in four equal 214 × 336 cards with 24 px gaps. Each dark card has a shared 198 × 268 image stage, a palette strip, and an aligned caption. All preview photos use a centered 168 × 252 (2:3) crop, so portrait, square, and landscape sources have identical visible bounds. Inspection uses separate uncropped images. The color dropdown shows only its selected name, left-aligned, with a right-side arrow and accent-colored selection. The overview has no subtitle.

The overview has no automatic cycling or accent outline. The title is vertically centered with the dropdown and motion controls. There are no arrow buttons; every card starts at y=228 with its label at y=192. All three neighbors remain clickable, and keyboard arrows move focus between them. Closing inspection restores focus to its card and preserves the pause state.

Inspection retains the horizontal shutter reveal from AXD (220 ms opening, 180 ms closing) and PET’s dark background. A paper frame fits each uncropped photograph, with a small caption footer, shadow, and gentle floating motion inspired by axd-site’s project prints. Portrait, square, and landscape images fit a 392 × 392 image area; the frame is centered in the left column. Badge assets are sized at export so JPEG decoding does not need runtime scaling.

The detail composition centers the photograph on y=340 and lowers the wheel and five-row legend to y=382. Titles and the palette label use consistent y=140 and y=196 positions across every photo. The circular palette adapts from 216 to 288 px according to photograph height and remaining width; the combined group is centered horizontally. Segment angles remain proportional to the five color weights, with no redundant center label. The legend uses five 60 px rows (300 px overall), giving the hex values and percentages more vertical breathing room. Pointer clicks do not show a green focus ring; keyboard navigation retains its focus indicator. Neighbor distance sits in the top bar, 24 px to the left of Close. The frame-to-wheel gap is 48 px and the wheel-to-legend gap is 40 px. Stopped motion, device motion/power settings, and browser reduced-motion preferences suppress frame animation. The browser supports Escape and restores focus to the selected photo.

`scripts/prepare-images.py` exports the unwatermarked originals from `perceptual-palette-drift/data/raw` and the original nearest-neighbor data. Each device photo now has a baseline JPEG thumbnail and a separate larger JPEG for inspection, decoded by LVGL's SJPG decoder with six cache entries. Decoded photos are released when leaving Constellation. This keeps the existing 4 MiB app partition; no partition or saved-data migration is needed.

Validation: `.local/check-constellation-adaptive.cjs` checks all 16 photographs at three browser widths, uniform preview crops and original inspection aspect ratios, fitted frame padding, wheel/legend separation, dark background, arrow removal, motion settings, and dismissal/focus behavior. The native LVGL renderer passes its AddressSanitizer smoke suite. Hardware flashing and physical touch/performance verification remain pending.
